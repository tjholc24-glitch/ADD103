Spider-Man: Brand New Day Review

The Story:

Peter Parker after the events of Spider-Man: No Way Home, is completely alone and has no social life. He is struggling to balance being Spider-Man and being himself, Peter Parker. Spider-Man in this movie teams up with the Punisher to try and take down Jean Grey, a telepathic mutant who is hunting down the people who killed her sister. During this hunt, Spider-Man undergoes his own mutation, which enhances his own arachnid abilities but takes away his emotional control. Spider-Man in this movie gets pushed to his mental limits as he trys to find the balance of being Peter and Spider-Man; he eventually finds his balance and convinces Jean to stop.

The Verdict:

Overall, I really enjoyed this movie; I’ve seen it 3 times already. A lot of the scenes between Spider-Man and his former love interest were very relatable. One of the things I really enjoyed was the action sequences; it felt like they were ripped straight from the video game. I give this movie an 8.6/10.
